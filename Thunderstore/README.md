# Smart Stations

Production stations open like a chest instead of "click to add one".

- **Input**, **Fuel** and **Products** slots for smelters, charcoal kilns, blast furnaces, eitr refineries, windmills, spinning wheels (any Smelter-based station, including modded ones) and the fermenter.
- Drag an item from your inventory onto the window, or right-click it, to add it. Click a slot to take everything back, right-click to take one.
- Products stay inside the station instead of dropping on the ground (configurable, capacity 100).
- **Auto fill**: each station can pull ore and fuel from nearby chests (10 m). Off by default, toggled per station with a button in its window.
- Shift + E keeps the vanilla interaction.
- Multiplayer safe: only the network owner of a station changes it, items are never duplicated. Every player needs the mod.

Requires BepInExPack Valheim and Jötunn.
