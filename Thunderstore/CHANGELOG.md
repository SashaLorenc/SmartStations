# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Slot window (input / fuel / products) for every Smelter-based station (charcoal kiln, smelter, blast furnace, eitr refinery, windmill, spinning wheel), the fermenter, cooking stations (cooking station, iron cooking station, stone oven) and output-only stations (beehive, sap collector).
- Drag and drop or right-click from the inventory, click a slot to take items back.
- Smelter products stay in the station instead of dropping on the ground.
- Per-station auto fill from nearby chests, toggled with a button (fuel only for cooking stations, so food never burns unattended).
- Multiplayer safe, Valheim 1.0 compatible.
